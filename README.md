# E-Commerce Sales Dashboard

## Overview

This project presents an interactive E-Commerce Sales Dashboard designed to analyze and visualize sales data. The dashboard transforms raw transactional data into meaningful insights that support business decision-making.

## Objectives

* Monitor overall business performance.
* Analyze product sales and customer behavior.
* Identify top-performing products.
* Track order status and payment methods.
* Visualize sales trends over time.

## Dashboard Features

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* Total Customers
* Total Orders
* Total Products
* Total Sales

### Product Analysis

* Top Products by Quantity Sold
* Top Products by Sales Revenue

### Customer Analysis

* Top Customer Information
* Customer purchase behavior insights

### Payment Analysis

* Sales distribution by payment method

### Order Analysis

* Comparison between delivered and cancelled orders

### Trend Analysis

* Monthly order trends and seasonal patterns

## Key Insights

* Chair is the highest-performing product in terms of quantity and sales.
* Credit Card is the most preferred payment method.
* June recorded the highest number of orders.
* Delivered orders slightly exceed cancelled orders.
* A small group of customers contributes significantly to total revenue.

## Recommendations

* Increase inventory for top-selling products.
* Improve customer retention through loyalty programs.
* Reduce order cancellations by improving fulfillment processes.
* Focus marketing campaigns on high-revenue products.
* Leverage seasonal sales patterns for better planning and forecasting.

## Tools Used

* Power BI
* Data Visualization Techniques
* Business Intelligence Concepts

## Conclusion

This dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of sales performance, customer behavior, and business trends. The insights generated can help organizations make data-driven decisions and improve overall operational efficiency.
